Arms Race For Natural Selection

counterfeit world where organisms(prey and predator) are in an arms-race to develop mechanisms to survive.

When I started this project 2 years ago, I had very little idea of what I wanted to do and how to get there. I also did not get the motivation to start it somewhere so I just let it rest.
But now I feel like I'm in a better place to understand evolution and what I really want.

I want to create a natural like evolving planet, where organisms fight each other in as close to nature a way as possible. starting with very very basic mechanisms. I want to understand how basic physics and chemistry create pressure for natural evolution and selection. This is just a random text dump but that is the general Idea. I will refine this.
